DIGITAL FORENSICS EXAMINATION REPORT
Examiner: Dr. Nathan Cross, Lead Digital Forensics Specialist
Case: Meridian Tech Server Room B Breach
Date of Analysis: October 13, 2024

1. PHYSICAL SECURITY FINDINGS
Physical inspection revealed the door latch pin was taped down with electrical tape, leaving the room open and accessible without authorization. The magnetic lock failed to engage because of the black vinyl electrical tape covering the sensor contact.

2. HARDWARE & CONSOLE INSPECTION
Console 2 in Server Room B was examined for digital traces:
- At 22:18:12, an active administrative session under username 'avance_admin' was used to query the customer database.
- At 22:20:45, a mass storage USB device was physically inserted into front USB port 1. Device identity: Kingston DataTraveler 3.0 (Serial: KTX-9941-B).
- At 22:31:18, an unencrypted archive 'customer_vault_export.tar.gz' (4.2 GB) was written directly to the USB drive.
- No outbound external network connection was established; the data was copied locally to a high-speed Kingston USB 3.0 flash drive directly mounted at Console 2.
- The USB drive was unmounted cleanly at 22:37:55.

3. LATENT EVIDENCE
Latent fingerprints lifted from Console 2 keyboard and the electrical tape roll match contractor David Wright.
