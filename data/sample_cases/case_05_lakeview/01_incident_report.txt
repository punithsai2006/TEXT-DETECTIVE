DOCUMENT 1: INITIAL INCIDENT REPORT
Document Type: Incident Report
Case ID: LV-3112
Author: Inspector Laura Bennett
Date: 22 November 2026

At 08:05 AM on 22 November 2026, head librarian Sarah Collins reported that an antique survey map, asset tag LV-MAP-07 ("Map of Lakeview Township, 1824"), was missing from the Rare Maps Room of Lakeview Public Library.

The map was last confirmed present at 03:34 PM on 21 November 2026. The library closed to the public at 05:00 PM on 21 November.

The Rare Maps Room has one door controlled by staff access cards, an alarm panel, and a locked display case with two keys. KEY_01 is held by Sarah Collins. KEY_02 is held by archivist Tom Reyes. No forced entry was found on the door or the display case.

Staff with access to the Rare Maps Room are Sarah Collins, Tom Reyes, and volunteer Nina Patel. Custodian Greg Hall works in the building in the evenings but has no access to the Rare Maps Room.

The map is valued at about 45,000 USD. No sale or transfer of the map has been confirmed at the time of this report.
