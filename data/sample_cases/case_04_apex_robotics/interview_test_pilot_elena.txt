Case: Apex Robotics Tactical Drone Crash
Interviewer: Agent Vance
Interviewee: Elena Rostova, Chief Flight Test Pilot

[10:30 AM] VANCE: Elena, what did you observe prior to the 21:00 UTC test flight?
[10:30 AM] ROSTOVA: I conducted the visual pre-flight inspection of SkyHarrier-9 at 20:00 UTC. The airframe was pristine.
[10:31 AM] VANCE: Did you see anyone approach the drone in Hangar 3?

[10:32 AM] VANCE: What happened during the test flight at 21:05 UTC?
