INTERVIEW TRANSCRIPT: JULIAN VANCE
Case: Blackwood Manor Art Heist
Interviewer: Detective Reynolds
Interviewee: Julian Vance, Night Guard

[10:30 AM] REYNOLDS: Julian, describe your patrol route on Friday night.
[10:30 AM] VANCE: I began my shift at 7:00 PM. I stayed in the security control booth until 11:00 PM.
[10:31 AM] REYNOLDS: Did you hear the basement motion alarm at 10:15 PM?
[10:31 AM] VANCE: The motion alarm never sounded in the booth. The control panel showed green all night.
[10:32 AM] REYNOLDS: Did anyone visit the estate during your watch?
[10:32 AM] VANCE: The courier van from Apex Deliveries arrived at 9:50 PM with conservatory plants, and drove off at 10:30 PM.
[10:33 AM] REYNOLDS: Did you leave your desk at all?
[10:33 AM] VANCE: I remained at the security console continuously from 7:00 PM to midnight without leaving.
