METROPOLITAN POLICE - INCIDENT REPORT
Incident: Burglary / Theft of High-Value Cultural Property
Location: Blackwood Manor, Surrey
Officer: DI Thomas Reynolds, Badge #8841
Date of Incident: October 18, 2024

3. FORENSICS: Tire tracks in the gravel courtyard match standard commercial light transport vehicles.
